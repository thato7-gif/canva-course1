🖥️ Session 2: Canva Basics

Get comfortable with the tool you'll use all day

Setup Checklist

  1. Go to canva.com and click Sign up.
  2. Use your email or sign in with Google — it's free.
  3. Skip any "Canva Pro" popups — you only need the free plan.
  4. Click Create a design → search "Instagram Post".
  5. You now have a blank 1080×1080 canvas ready to work on.

Deeper Dive

🧭 Tour of the Canva Interface
  • Left Sidebar — Templates, Elements, Text, Uploads, Photos
  • Top Toolbar — File, Resize, Share, Download
  • Centre Canvas — Where your design appears
  • Context Bar — Appears when you select something
⭐ The 5 Things You'll Use Most
  1. Templates — Starting points for inspiration
  2. Elements — Shapes, icons, stickers, lines
  3. Text — Heading, Subheading, Body text (stick to 2 fonts)
  4. Uploads — Your own images
  5. Position tools — Align, space evenly, transparency

▶️ Practice — Your First Design

  1. Create a new "Instagram Post" (1080×1080).
  2. Choose a template from "Social Media" that you like.
  3. Change the headline to your own name.
  4. Change the subheading to "Future Designer".
  5. Swap the photo for one from the Photos tab.
  6. Click Share → Download → PNG to save it.
