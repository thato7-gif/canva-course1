🧭 Session 1: Design Foundations

The 6 principles that make design work

Icebreaker

"What's the last poster, logo, or Instagram post that made you stop scrolling — and why?"

The 6 Principles

1. Contrast

Make important things stand out. Dark vs light, big vs small.

2. Alignment

Everything lines up on invisible grid lines.

3. Repetition

Reuse the same colours, fonts, and shapes for unity.

4. Proximity

Related items go together. Unrelated items get space.

5. Balance

Weight is distributed evenly.

6. White Space

Empty space lets your content breathe.

Deeper Dive

💡 Why Design Matters for You
Your CV is a design. Your LinkedIn is a design. Your brand is a design. Employers and clients judge quality in under 3 seconds. Even a simple, well-designed poster looks more professional than a crowded one.
🔍 How to Spot Bad Design
Look for: too many fonts, clashing colours, uneven spacing, elements that don't align, no clear focal point, and text that's too small or low-contrast.

▶️ Practice — Spot the Problem

Read the scenario and type which principle is being broken.
