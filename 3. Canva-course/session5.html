🏷️ Session 5: Logo & Social Media Kit

Design a brand identity you can use everywhere

What Makes a Great Logo?

  • Simple — Recognisable at 16px or on a billboard.
  • Memorable — Works in black and white too.
  • Timeless — Doesn't follow every trend.
  • Versatile — Business card, T-shirt, app icon.
  • Relevant — Fits the brand's tone.

Deeper Dive

🏷️ The 3 Logo Types
  • Wordmark — Just the name in a custom font (Google, Coca-Cola)
  • Lettermark — Initials only (HBO, IBM, KFC)
  • Icon + Text — Symbol paired with the name (Nike, Apple, Spotify)
📱 The 3-Piece Social Media Kit
  1. Profile picture — 1080×1080 (square, simple)
  2. Cover / banner — 1500×500 (LinkedIn, Facebook)
  3. Post template — 1080×1080 (Instagram, quote card)
All three use the same colours, fonts, and shapes — that makes it a kit.

▶️ The Challenge — Build Your Personal Logo

Logo Checklist
  • 1080×1080 canvas
  • One or two fonts
  • Two colours max
  • Readable at 50% zoom
  • Works in black & white
Social Kit Checklist
  • Profile picture uses same logo
  • Banner uses same colours
  • Post template uses same fonts
  • All 3 pieces feel like one brand
