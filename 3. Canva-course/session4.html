📢 Session 4: Designing a Poster

From blank canvas to finished event poster

The 5 Layers of a Poster

  1. Background — colour, gradient, or photo
  2. Hero image — the main visual
  3. Headline — 3–6 words, biggest text
  4. Details — date, time, location, price
  5. Call to action — website, QR code, contact

Deeper Dive

✅ The Poster Golden Rules
  • Readable in 3 seconds from 3 metres away.
  • One focal point — the eye should know where to start.
  • Maximum 2 fonts.
  • Maximum 3 colours (+ black & white).
  • Never stretch images — hold Shift when resizing.

Your Poster Checklist

Design

  • 2 fonts max
  • 3 colours max
  • Clear focal point
  • No stretched images
Content
  • Headline (3–6 words)
  • Date + Time + Location
  • One-line description
  • Contact / website / QR
  • Consistent spacing
