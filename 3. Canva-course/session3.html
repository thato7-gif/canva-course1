🎨 Session 3: Colour & Typography

The two most powerful tools in your design kit

Colour Theory in 5 Minutes

  • Primary colours — Red, Yellow, Blue. Can't be mixed.
  • Secondary colours — Green, Orange, Purple. Mixed from two primaries.
  • Complimentary colours — Opposite on the wheel (blue + orange). High contrast.
  • Analogous colours — Next to each other (blue + teal). Calm, harmonious.
  • 60-30-10 rule — 60% main, 30% secondary, 10% accent.

Deeper Dive

🏆 The 3 Golden Rules of Font Pairing
  1. Use 2 fonts max — one bold for headings, one clean for body.
  2. Serif + Sans-Serif pairs beautifully (Playfair + Open Sans).
  3. Never use two decorative fonts together — they fight.
🎯 How to Pick a Palette Fast
Pick one base colour. Choose an accent that contrasts (complimentary). Add a neutral (black, white, or grey). That's your whole palette — 3 colours.
