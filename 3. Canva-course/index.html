🎨 Basic Graphic Design with Canva

A One-Day Creative Workshop for Grade 12 Students

Workshop Overview

What You Will Learn
  • The 6 core principles of good design
  • How to use Canva from scratch (free account)
  • Colour theory + pairing fonts like a pro
  • Design a real event poster from a blank canvas
  • Create a logo + 3-piece social media kit
Workshop Details
  • Duration: 1 day (~6 hours)
  • Audience: Grade 12, no design experience needed
  • Outcome: A poster, a logo, and a social media kit
  • Tools: Laptop + free Canva account
💡 Tip: You don't need any art skills — Canva gives you professional templates. You'll learn to use them well.

What's Inside

📚 Session 1 — Design Foundations
The 6 principles of design: contrast, alignment, repetition, proximity, balance, and white space. Learn to spot good design instantly.
🖥️ Session 2 — Canva Basics
Set up your free Canva account, tour the interface, and build your first Instagram post from scratch.
🎨 Session 3 — Colour & Typography
Colour theory, the 60-30-10 rule, font pairing, and the 3 golden rules of typography.
📢 Session 4 — Designing a Poster
Design a real event poster using the 5 layers of a poster and the 3-second readability rule.
🏷️ Session 5 — Logo & Social Media Kit
Design a personal logo plus a 3-piece social media kit that shares consistent colours and fonts.
